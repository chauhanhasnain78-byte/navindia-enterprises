Place your .glb 3D product models inside this folder (/public/models/) using these exact filenames:

1. Hoses:
   hoses.glb

2. Fasteners:
   fasteners.glb

3. Seals and O-Rings:
   seals-and-o-rings.glb

4. Abrasives:
   abrasives.glb

5. Lifting Products:
   lifting-products.glb

6. MS/SS Industrial Products:
   ms-ss-industrial-products.glb

7. Other Industrial Supplies:
   other-industrial-supplies.glb

As soon as any of these .glb files is placed in /public/models/, the website will automatically load, center, auto-scale, and spin that real 3D model in the category card!
